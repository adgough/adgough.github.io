BAHsim - Bolin PTZ controller

Sim for "Camera operator"

XBox controller

An Xbox controller may be used with this sim:

  • PTZ joystick = XBox right analog stick.
  • PTZ zoom in/out rocker buttons = XBox left/right Dpad buttons.

Enable sleep sim

Enable simulation of the PTZ controller going into standby after 10 seconds of no user input. Takes 3 seconds to wake up after a button press. It's not realistic to turn this off, but might be handy if you're just trying to get used to what buttons do.
